Marketing work often starts clearly enough. Someone needs a landing page, a campaign, a newsletter or a case study. Then the work spreads out.

The brief lives in a chat thread. The draft is in a document. Comments arrive by email. Brand or legal approval depends on someone remembering to chase. Publishing happens in another tool, and nobody is quite sure what is live.

The cost is not a missing idea. It is the coordination around the idea.

## What a marketing operations workflow can hold

A useful system does not replace the writing, the design or the judgement. It holds the path those things travel on.

- A brief arrives in one place, with the channel, owner and due date attached
- Drafts stay connected to the original request instead of becoming a new document each time
- Brand, legal or founder review is a named step, not a favour asked in Slack
- Ready work can be prepared for the publishing tools the team already uses
- Status is visible without a weekly round-up spreadsheet

The aim is a queue the team can run on a normal Tuesday: what is in draft, what is waiting, what is approved, and what has gone out.

## Approvals stay with people

Marketing automation goes wrong when it tries to publish without a person.

A controlled workflow can draft, remind, route and prepare. It should not send a campaign, change a live page or speak for the brand unless someone with authority has approved it.

That is the same control principle Layer uses in operations and finance. The system moves the repetitive coordination. People keep the decisions that affect customers and reputation.

## Designed around the tools you already use

Most marketing teams already have enough software. Email, a CRM, documents, a website CMS, social tools and a shared inbox.

The missing layer is usually the workflow between them: intake, draft, review, publish, and a record of what happened.

The example above is a starting pattern, not a product you have to adopt. The useful version is the one shaped around how your team already briefs, reviews and ships work.

## The practical aim

Reduce the chasing around marketing work. Make it obvious where a brief is, who needs to look at it, and what is allowed to go out.
