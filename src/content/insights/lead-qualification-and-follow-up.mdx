Inbound enquiries are easy to receive and easy to lose.

A form submission, a website message or a forwarded email arrives. Someone means to reply. The CRM is updated later, or not at all. Follow-up depends on memory. A good-fit company waits while a poor-fit enquiry gets a same-day response because it happened to be at the top of the inbox.

The problem is not that the team does not care. It is that the process still depends on a person acting as the integration layer between the inbox, the CRM and the calendar.

## What the workflow can do

A lead qualification system is useful when it makes the next step obvious.

- Capture the enquiry from the form, inbox or website
- Attach the company context the team already has, or can reasonably enrich
- Assess fit against simple rules the business already uses
- Draft a response for review, rather than leaving a blank reply
- Create or update the CRM record so the conversation does not live in one person's head
- Schedule the follow-up so it happens even when the week gets busy

Straightforward enquiries can move quickly. Ambiguous or high-value conversations still wait for a person.

## Fit is a rule, not a vibe

Qualification should be boring and inspectable.

The business already knows, roughly, who it can help: company type, size, geography, the kind of operational problem, whether there is a process owner. Those rules can sit in the workflow.

The system can recommend High, Medium or Low fit and explain why. It should not silently discard an enquiry, and it should not send a polished reply that the team has not seen.

> **No auto-send by default.** The system prepares the record and the draft. A person decides what goes out.

## Follow-up is part of the workflow

Most lost enquiries are not lost at first contact. They are lost in the gap after it.

A controlled workflow can create the next task, remind the owner, and keep the thread attached to the company record. That is ordinary operational discipline, not a sales methodology.

If the team already uses HubSpot, a shared inbox, or a spreadsheet that has become a pipeline, the system should connect those tools rather than ask everyone to move house.

## The practical aim

Make inbound work visible, qualified and followed up, without turning the first conversation into an automated pitch.
